# The Ferryman's Apprentice

A top-down 3D soulslike/metroidvania set in a flooded underworld: the Apprentice takes up the vanished
Ferryman's oar to find out why the dead have stopped crossing.

## Language

### Characters

**Apprentice**:
The player character: the Ferryman's small, masked apprentice.
_Avoid_: Hero, player (when meaning the character)

**Ferryman**:
The Apprentice's vanished master, who stopped letting the dead cross and began hoarding them.
_Avoid_: Master, boatman

**Soul**:
Any of the dead; the umbrella term for Lingerers and the Soured.
_Avoid_: Spirit, ghost

**Lingerer**:
A soul held back by unfinished business, who can cross once it's resolved and their fare is paid.
_Avoid_: NPC, lost soul, quest giver

**Keepsake**:
Something a Lingerer lost, found out in the world and carried back to them; bringing it resolves their business. Not Equipment, never sold.
_Avoid_: Quest item, key item (that's an Ability's)

**Fare**:
The coin a Lingerer's Crossing costs, paid by the Apprentice in Obols once their business is resolved. Always optional.
_Avoid_: Toll, fee, price

**Soured**:
A soul the Ferryman kept too long, turned hostile; the game's enemies. Defeat scatters them, it doesn't free them.
_Avoid_: Enemy, monster, hollow

**Soured family**:
A kind of Soured defined by what it couldn't let go of: the Laden, the Clinger, the Keener, the Moths, the Drowned.
_Avoid_: Enemy type, species, class

**Laden archer**:
A Laden that let most of its heap go and kept a bow: shoots single arrows or a volley of three from range, and keeps its distance. A variant within the Laden family, not a family of its own.
_Avoid_: Archer (alone), ranged Laden, bowman

**Crossing**:
Rowing a Lingerer from the hub jetty to the Far shore, releasing them. On the water they speak (a memory of the Ferryman); at the Far shore they go on into its light and leave a parting gift.
_Avoid_: Release, passing, ascension

### Progression

**Oar**:
The Apprentice's only weapon and tool, used to fight, row and traverse.
_Avoid_: Weapon, paddle, staff

**Ability**:
A permanent new oar technique that has both a combat use and a traversal use.
_Avoid_: Skill, power, upgrade

**Hook**, **Stroke**, **Vault**:
The three Abilities: the Hook pulls you to a hook post, yanks a Soured over to you (a Boss too heavy for that: it jerks the Anchoress's anchor, her guard gone a while); a rowing stroke that sends a wave; plant the oar and leap.

**Ability gate**:
An obstacle, on land or on water, that can only be passed once the Apprentice has a specific Ability.
_Avoid_: Lock, barrier

**Oar step**:
How strong the Oar is: five in order, each with its own look and name (the Apprentice's, the Oarhand's, the Boatman's, the Steersman's, the Ferryman's Oar). Strengthened at a Mooring post with Ferry Nails, never skipped. There is only ever one Oar.
_Avoid_: Weapon level, upgrade, new oar, Oar attachment (retired)

**Ferry Nail**:
A find: an old boat nail from the ferries. Spent at a Mooring post to strengthen the Oar.
_Avoid_: Upgrade material, titanite, shard

**Warm Stone**:
A find that adds health for good.
_Avoid_: Heart piece, HP upgrade

**Held Breath**:
A find that adds stamina for good.
_Avoid_: Stamina upgrade

**Equipment**:
The Charms the Apprentice owns and has fitted; never another weapon.
_Avoid_: Gear, loot, items

**Charm**:
A piece of passive Equipment that modifies the Apprentice without changing the Oar.
_Avoid_: Card, trinket, relic

**Charm slot**:
A place for one fitted Charm; the Apprentice starts with one and buys more. Charms are fitted only at a Mooring post.
_Avoid_: Socket, equip slot

**Merchant soul**:
The soul who sells Equipment and consumables for Obols; waits in the Hub.
_Avoid_: Shop, vendor, trader

**Bow lantern**:
The Apprentice's own lantern, carried at the hip and hung on the boat's bow when rowing; its cold light grows with each lit Mooring post and burns through fog on the water. Never an Ability.
_Avoid_: Lamp, light

**Lantern level**:
How far the Bow lantern has grown, 1 to 5, from the Mooring posts lit across the whole journey. Fully lit, it opens the way to the secret last Boss and ending.
_Avoid_: Lamp level, light level

**Character screen**:
The paused screen with six pages: the Apprentice, the Oar, Abilities, the Satchel, Charms and the Lantern.
_Avoid_: Inventory, status menu

**Provision**:
Something carried and used up: a Pitch Pot, Ember Tar, the Way Back, Warm Broth, a Bitter Root. Bought or found, each kind up to a cap; Resting doesn't refill them.
_Avoid_: Consumable, item, potion

**Poison**:
What a Drowned's spit leaves in you: a build-up that ebbs if you keep clear; full, you're poisoned, losing health a while, until it wears off or is cured (a Bitter Root, a Rest).
_Avoid_: Toxin, venom, status

**Satchel**:
Where the Apprentice carries Provisions, and the character screen's page for them.
_Avoid_: Inventory, bag

**Belt**:
The four slots Provisions are used from, each holding one kind (or none): left/right choose along it, passing empty slots, up uses the chosen one.
_Avoid_: Quick slots, hotbar

**Obol**:
The currency: the fare the dead carry to pay for crossing.
_Avoid_: Coin, money, gold, souls (as currency)

### Combat

**Healing charge**:
One use of the Apprentice's limited self-heal; the carried charges refill only by Resting.
_Avoid_: Potion, flask, estus

**Stagger meter**:
A hidden per-enemy gauge that hits fill (light a little, heavy a lot) and that drains over time; when full, the enemy is staggered.
_Avoid_: Poise bar, stun meter

**Perfect block**:
A block raised just before a blow lands (within 0.2 s): it takes no damage and always stuns the attacker.
_Avoid_: Parry, deflect

**Riposte**:
A hit on a stunned Soured: double damage, straight through any guard.
_Avoid_: Critical, counter

**Telegraph**:
The readable wind-up of an attack, shown through the attacker's animation, never through ground markers.
_Avoid_: Tell, warning, indicator

**Shade**:
The ghost of the Apprentice left where they died, holding the Obols they carried.
_Avoid_: Corpse, ghost, bloodstain, echo

**Boss**:
The unique, tough enemy at the climax of an Area.

**Old Hesper**:
The Drowned City's Lingerer, in full "Old Hesper, who keeps a light": an old woman on the landing keeping a lamp lit for her son, who went out the night the water came up. Her keepsake is his boat-lantern, still burning out on the islet north of the yard (reached with the Hook); brought it, she can let him go. Her fare paid, she waits by the boat for her Crossing; rowed to the Far shore, she leaves his lantern on its jetty and gives the Apprentice Hesper's Wick.

**Miniboss**:
A lesser Boss within an Area, met on the way: its own name plate, bar and farewell like a Boss, but no Ability and no Area's end. The Drowned City's is the Unparted; the Orchard's, the Watchman.
_Avoid_: Elite, sub-boss, mid-boss

**The Unparted**:
The Drowned City's miniboss, in full "The Unparted, of the Last Embrace": two souls who never let each other go, grown into one great hunched Clinger. Hold carries; Fast rides its back, leaping down to cling to the Apprentice and climbing back up. Scatter Fast first and Hold goes berserk; scatter Hold first and Fast weeps where it fell.

**The Watchman**:
The Orchard's miniboss, in full "The Watchman, of the Unreaped Rows": the man who worked the orchard, a scarecrow among scarecrows in the Unreaped Rows, keeping watch over a harvest that never came. He moves only while he isn't watched. First he must be found (he creeps when he's behind you); then faced down.

**The Unreaped Rows**:
The field above the Orchard, through the gateway in its wall, never harvested: the Watchman's arena. Its gates shut behind you while his fight is on.

**Scarecrow**:
Straw in an old coat on a stake, in the Unreaped Rows; the Watchman stands among them as one of them. Struck, one bursts, and the Moths nesting in its straw come out. Not a Soured (it has no soul), though it's struck like one.

**Watched**:
Faced by the Apprentice: in front of them, within 60° either side of where they face.

**The Antediluvian Anchoress**:
The Drowned City's Boss: a Laden who would not let the Ferryman go. She chained his ferry's anchor to herself (fastened with his stolen hook) and keeps vigil in the drowned chapel; beaten, she gives up the Hook. "Antediluvian": from before the flood. Her full title, revealed as her fight first begins: the Antediluvian Anchoress, of Fleeting Memories.
_Avoid_: the Hoarder (her old working name), the Bell-Keeper, the anchor boss

### World

**Area**:
A themed region of the underworld with its own Boss: the Drowned City, the Orchard of Last Autumn, the Stopped Clocktower.
_Avoid_: Level, zone, biome

**Hub**:
The Ferryman's jetty at the centre of the underworld, where Crossings leave from and the Ferryman's hoard lies beneath.
_Avoid_: Home, base, town

**Far shore**:
Where souls go when they cross. The Apprentice can row out to its jetty, but no further: the way on beyond its light is for the dead.
_Avoid_: Afterlife, other side

**Mooring post**:
A post on a jetty where the Apprentice moors the boat and Rests; the game's checkpoint.
_Avoid_: Checkpoint, bonfire, save point, shrine

**Shortcut**:
A way back towards a Mooring post, opened only from its far side (a barred gate, a lowered ladder); once opened it stays open.
_Avoid_: Door, unlock, short cut

**Ferryman's mark**:
An oar through a ring, inlaid on everything of the Ferryman's (hook posts, the Hook's relic): a sign that it's his, and that it connects.
_Avoid_: Sigil, rune, symbol

**Hook post**:
A pile with an iron ring that the Hook can pull the Apprentice to, across water; the first kind of Ability gate.
_Avoid_: Grapple point, anchor

**Ember**:
A find, hidden behind a secret, that adds one Healing charge for good.
_Avoid_: Healing charge piece, flask upgrade, estus shard

**Journey**:
One playthrough, kept in one of three save slots: chosen, begun or continued from the title screen, and saved as you go (every Rest, and whenever something that lasts changes).
_Avoid_: Save file, profile, run

**Rest**:
Stopping at a Mooring post: heals fully, refills Healing charges, saves, and respawns all enemies except defeated Bosses.
_Avoid_: Sit, save
