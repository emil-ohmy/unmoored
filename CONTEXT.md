# MyGame

A top-down 3D action game in the soulslike/metroidvania tradition: deliberate combat, an interconnected
world opened up by new abilities.

## Language

### Progression

**Ability**:
A permanent power the player gains that has both a combat use and a traversal use.
_Avoid_: Skill, power, upgrade

**Ability gate**:
An obstacle in the world that can only be passed once the player has a specific Ability.
_Avoid_: Lock, barrier

**Equipment**:
A weapon or item the player finds and equips that changes how they fight.
_Avoid_: Gear, loot, items

### Combat

**Healing charge**:
One use of the player's limited self-heal; the player carries a fixed number that refill only under specific rules.
_Avoid_: Potion, flask, estus

**Boss**:
The unique, tough enemy at the climax of an Area.

### World

**Area**:
A themed region of the world with its own Boss.
_Avoid_: Level, zone, biome
