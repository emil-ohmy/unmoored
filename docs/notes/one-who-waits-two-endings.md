# The One Who Waits: two endings

The user's direction (2026-11-01) for The One Who Waits, the Hall of the Last Hour's Boss:
- With the Hall's time stopped, he is limp and very easy. Killing him then leads to the bad ending.
- With the clock tower running (the central engine set going), he is much harder. Killing him then lets you continue
  further, toward the true ending.

Nothing past the Boss is to be designed yet; the endings are noted for later.

**Why:** the engine and the Hall's time are the Area's central choice, and this makes that choice decide the game's
ending.

**How to apply:** design and build the Boss fight in both states. Don't build the endings or anything beyond the Boss
until asked. Relates to [set pieces before progression](set-pieces-before-progression.md).
