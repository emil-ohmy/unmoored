# Creature done checklist

Every new creature or Boss must ship with, unasked: a swipe on every blow in reach (checked from the game camera, not
just present: the Hold's first fingertip swipe was an invisible hairline), its Ledger entry (tested by beating it as
played), lock-on to every part you strike (the Hold's hands weren't lockable), and numbers that suit its place (the
Hold, the first Boss, was far too tanky at 600 health). Later (2026-11-03) the user added: every Boss's fall gets the
moment (BossFall: hit-stop, slow time, the camera closing in; the Hold had none: not a Soured), its name comes up only
once it's on screen (the Hold's came up while the camera was still on the Apprentice), and its arena isn't a long
walk to where it can reach you.

**Why:** the user had to point each of these out after play-testing, twice for the Ledger (the One Who Waits, then
the Hold), and asked that swipes ALWAYS happen. CLAUDE.md carries the checklist.

**How to apply:** before reporting a creature or Boss as done, go through the CLAUDE.md checklist and say in the
report that each holds. See [set pieces before progression](set-pieces-before-progression.md).
