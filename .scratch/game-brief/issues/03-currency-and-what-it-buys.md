# What currency is for
Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 02

## Question

Progression leans towards abilities + equipment, with no stat upgrades. But both references DO sell capped
stat upgrades (see the research ticket), so first confirm: no stat upgrades at all, capped ones, or only found
ones (e.g. max-HP shards, extra Healing charges)? Then: is there a currency at all, and if so what does it buy
(equipment, charm/card slots, consumables, shortcuts...)? This decides what a death penalty can take from the player.

Context from the hook ticket: the concept pitch floated "souls you guide = currency", but "soul" also means the
NPC souls with unfinished business. If currency is souls, settle the term split in `CONTEXT.md`. The theme is
"letting go" and the Ferryman's sin is hoarding souls: consider whether spending currency should feel like
releasing, not hoarding.

## Answer

Resolved 2026-09-27 with the user (quiz format).

- **Stat upgrades: found only.** Nothing stat-like is for sale. Max HP and extra Healing charges come from pieces
  found in the world, often behind secrets.
- **Currency: Obols**, the fare of the dead (Charon's obol). Dropped by enemies and found in the world.
  "Soul" is reserved for the dead themselves, never the currency.
- **Obols buy**: Oar attachments (some sold, some found), Charms and Charm slots, consumables, and passage
  for souls. Spending on gear vs paying a soul's fare is a deliberate "keep or let go" tension.
- **What death can take**: Obols. How much and whether they're retrievable → Death, checkpoints and healing.
- Considered and not chosen: souls as currency (spending souls = the Ferryman's sin of hoarding); capped purchasable stats; no currency.
