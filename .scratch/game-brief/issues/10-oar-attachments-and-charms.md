# Oar attachments and Charms
Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by:

## Question

At brief level: how many Oar attachments and Charms does the game have, what kinds of change do they make (reach/speed/weight
for attachments; passive effects for Charms), how many Charm slots, which are found vs sold for Obols, and is the unlockable third
light swing one of them? Must not overlap with the Abilities (Hook, Stroke, Vault), and must respect "one oar, many uses".

## Answer

Resolved 2026-09-27 with the user (quiz format).

- **Oar attachments**: the Plain blade (the Ferryman's, from the start; balanced, the Middle pace) + 4 more. One fitted at a time,
  swapped at Mooring posts.
  - **Iron blade**: slower, much more stagger fill, bigger hit-stop.
  - **Light blade**: faster, cheaper stamina, less damage; carries the **third light swing** (3-swing combo; others keep 2).
  - **Long shaft**: more reach and wider sweeps, longer recovery.
  - **Barbed blade**: hits make the Soured's stagger meter drain much more slowly, so steady pressure pays off. Shield-ripping stays with the Hook.
  - **Sources**: 3 found (one per Area), 1 sold by the merchant soul at the hub.
- **Charms**: about 8, some with trade-offs. Start with 1 slot; 2 more bought from the merchant (3 max). Half found (secrets,
  Lingerer gifts), half sold. Swapped at Mooring posts.
- **Adjusts Souls with unfinished business**: Lingerer gifts are Charms or upgrade pieces only, never Oar attachments.
- Considered and not chosen: swap anytime; combinable attachment slots; 12 or 5 Charms; third swing as a Charm or permanent upgrade;
  Barbed as pull-closer; mostly-sold or mostly-found economies.
