# Soured families
Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by:

## Question

Which 3–5 Soured archetypes does the brief commit to (one line each)? For each: what it couldn't let go of in life
(its shape echoes that), its role in a fight (e.g. punishes mashing, keeps distance, swarms), how its Stagger meter
differs, and which Ability or Oar attachment it's especially vulnerable to. Telegraphs are animation-only (art test),
and the verbs, Abilities and attachments are known.

## Answer

Resolved 2026-09-27 with the user (quiz format).

- **Five families**, each a soul that couldn't let go of something; its shape shows what:
  - **The Laden** (things): carries a towering heap of belongings. Tank; the heap blocks frontal hits and punishes
    mashing. Huge Stagger meter. Weak to the Hook (rip the heap off).
  - **The Clinger** (a person): small, fast, in pairs; latches on and drains stamina. A roll throws it off (normal
    stamina cost), a Stroke peels off all of them; no mashing. Tiny Stagger meter. Weak to the Stroke.
  - **The Keener** (grief): keeps distance and wails; rings of sound roll across the ground and must be rolled through.
    Low Stagger meter, fragile. Weak to the Vault (over the rings and in).
  - **The Moths** (memories): a flock of small pale wisps that circle and dive, one hit each. No Stagger meter. Weak to
    the Long shaft and heavy sweeps.
  - **The Drowned** (life): rises at water edges and drags the Apprentice in. Being pulled under deals damage and
    washes the Apprentice up on the nearest solid ground; not a death, no Shade (keeps "no death on water").
    Medium Stagger meter. Weak to the Hook (yanked out of the water, it flounders).
- **Weaknesses are advantages, never requirements**: every family is beatable with the base verbs.
- **Placement**: one new family per Area. Drowned Village: Laden + Clinger (Drowned at water edges everywhere, all game).
  Orchard of Last Autumn: + Moths. Stopped Clocktower: + Keener, mixed with all earlier ones.
- Considered and not chosen: the Grudge, the Waiting; 3 or 4 families; both water enemies weak to Stroke; required
  weaknesses; hit-off/timed-release Clinger; water as death; themed-only or all-everywhere placement.
