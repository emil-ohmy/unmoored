# World structure: hub, areas, bosses, shortcuts
Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 01, 04, 07

## Question

How is the flooded underworld laid out at brief level: what is the hub (it has a jetty that Crossings to the far shore leave from), what are the
2–3 Areas (islands? theme + Boss each), how does rowing connect them, where do land and water Ability gates and
shortcuts go, where do secrets fit (found max-HP and Healing-charge pieces live there), where are Obols spent, where do Mooring posts sit (moderate density, one before each Boss), is there fast travel between posts or is rowing the only way, and where is the Ferryman's final confrontation?

Inputs from the abilities ticket: 3 Areas implied (Boss 1 → Hook, Boss 2 → Stroke, Boss 3 → access to the Ferryman). Vault hides
behind a secret in Area 1 and is required in Area 3. Critical-path water gates are currents (Stroke) and posts over water (Hook);
fog routes are optional and scale with the bow lantern (which grows per lit Mooring post; all lit → secret ending).

## Answer

Resolved 2026-09-27 with the user (quiz format).

- **Hub: the Ferryman's jetty.** A small island at the centre: his hut, the big jetty Crossings leave from, Lingerers waiting on
  the shore, a merchant soul who sells for Obols, one Mooring post.
- **Layout: spokes that link up.** Each Area is reached by rowing from the hub; neighbouring Areas also connect through
  shortcuts and hidden routes, so the player can loop hub → Area → Area → hub. Links open from the far side.
- **No fast travel.** Rowing and shortcuts are the travel. Each Boss arena opens a shortcut back towards the hub.
- **Areas, in order**:
  1. **The Drowned Village**: open from the start, no gates on the critical path. Vault hidden on the rooftops (secret).
     Boss: **The Hoarder**, a lumbering heap of furniture and keepsakes around a small soul. Reward: Hook.
  2. **The Orchard of Last Autumn**: reached by Hooking across posts and branches over water; forever late autumn,
     warm colours. Boss: **The Gardener**, rooted in the oldest tree, refusing to let the harvest end. Reward: Stroke.
  3. **The Stopped Clocktower**: reached by Stroking up a strong current; vertical, Vault required inside. Boss:
     **The One Who Waits**, bound to the clock's gears. Reward: access to the Ferryman.
- **Finale beneath the hub**: after Boss 3 the water around the jetty drains, revealing the Ferryman's hoard under it. Final Boss: the Ferryman.
- **In every Area**: a few Mooring posts (one before the Boss), about 2 Lingerers, secrets with max-HP and Healing-charge pieces,
  optional fog routes for the bow lantern.
- **Risk noted**: the Orchard's trees/foliage are the hardest thing to script in Blender; added to the art test.
- Considered and not chosen: drowned-village or lighthouse hub; pure spokes; one interconnected whole; any fast travel;
  Sunken Post Office, Lantern Marsh, Wreck Graveyard, Seaside Fair, Library of Unfinished Books; finale on a hidden island or the far shore.
