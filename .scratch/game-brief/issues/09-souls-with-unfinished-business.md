# Souls with unfinished business
Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 03

## Question

How does "letting go" show up in play? What do the NPC souls with unfinished business want, how does the
Apprentice help release them (a quest, a delivery, a fight, a choice), what does releasing one give the player,
and roughly how many does the brief commit to? Must not overlap confusingly with whatever currency turns out to be.

From the currency ticket: Obols can pay passage for souls, competing with buying gear. Decide whether paying a
soul's fare is optional (a moral/reward choice) or sometimes required for progress, and what the Apprentice gets back.
