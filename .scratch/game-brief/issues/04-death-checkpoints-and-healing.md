# Death, checkpoints and healing
Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 02, 03

## Question

When the Apprentice dies: where do they come back, how many Obols do they lose, can they get them back (corpse run)?
Where do limited healing charges refill? Is there a checkpoint/rest point (the user didn't pick
"checkpoints + enemy respawn", but healing charges need a refill point), and do enemies respawn?
Extra Healing charges are found in the world (currency ticket), so this ticket only decides how carried charges refill.

## Answer

Resolved 2026-09-27 with the user (quiz format).

- **Rest point: Mooring posts.** The Apprentice moors at a jetty's post and lights its lantern. Resting fully heals,
  refills Healing charges and saves.
- **Density: moderate.** A few per Area, always one right before a Boss. Opening shortcuts back to posts is a key
  exploration reward.
- **On death**: wake at the last Mooring post rested at. **All enemies respawn except defeated Bosses**, on death and on rest.
- **Obols on death**: all carried Obols are dropped with the Apprentice's **Shade** where they fell. Touch the
  Shade to get them back; die again first and they're gone.
- **Healing charges**: 3 at the start, refilled only by resting at a Mooring post; found pieces add more.
- **Edge cases**: dying in a Boss fight places the Shade just outside the arena. No death on water, so the Shade
  is always on land. A new death replaces an unclaimed Shade (its Obols are lost).
- Supersedes the charting leaning "no checkpoints with enemy respawn".
- Considered and not chosen: lanterns/grave shrines as rest points; no or partial respawn; partial Obol loss.
