# Abilities and the gates they open
Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 01, 05

## Question

Which 2–3 oar techniques (Abilities) does the game have? Each must have both a combat use and a traversal use
(to open an Ability gate, on land or on water), and must build on the combat verbs (move, i-frame roll, 2-swing light, heavy, stamina, stagger meter; see
the combat ticket). In what order are they
found, and how (bosses, souls, the Ferryman's traces)?

## Answer

Resolved 2026-09-27 with the user (quiz format).

- **Three Abilities (oar techniques)**:
  - **Hook**, from Boss 1. Traversal: pull to posts across water/gaps, pull levers and floating objects. Combat: yank Soured close, rip off shields.
  - **Stroke**, from Boss 2. Traversal: row against currents, push the boat and objects. Combat: a wave that knocks Soured back or off ledges.
  - **Vault**, hidden behind a secret in Area 1. Traversal: over gaps and low walls, onto ledges. Combat: leap over attacks, slam down (big stagger fill).
- **Pattern**: two from Bosses, one secret (the user's choice). Vault is optional early (shortcuts, sequence breaks) and required in
  Area 3. Reaching Area 3 without it, the gate there hints where it is, so the player is never stuck.
- **The last Boss** grants access to the Ferryman, not a technique.
- **Bow lantern** (moved off the Ability list to keep "one oar"): not an Ability and never required to finish. Carried from the
  start, dim; grows stronger with every Mooring post lit. Thicker fog on the water hides optional routes and secrets. A fully powered
  lantern (every Mooring post lit) unlocks a **secret ending** (the user's idea).
- **Critical-path water gates**: currents (Stroke) and posts across water (Hook) only. Fog never gates progress.
- Considered and not chosen: Lantern as an Ability; 4 Abilities; 2 Abilities; all from Bosses; first found then Bosses.
